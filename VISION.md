# Vision

Trading Max turns a person's read-only brokerage data into a local,
auditable portfolio and research workspace.

## Principles

- Local-first: state and credentials remain under the operating-system user.
- Read-only: the product does not place trades.
- Explainable: numbers retain provenance and missing data fails visibly.
- Recoverable: immutable snapshots, migrations, backups, and rollback protect
  the research record.
- Focused: one well-supported local deployment is better than broad,
  unverified platform claims.

See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for current scope and explicit non-goals.
